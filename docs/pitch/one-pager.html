AI Builder Cup 2026 · Manufacturing

One button takes a night-shift supervisor from line stoppage to root cause, with evidence anyone can check.

給中小代工廠夜班主管的停線調查 Copilot，一鍵找根因，每個結論都查得到證據。

Problem

3 a.m., Line 2 stops. The night supervisor is alone; engineers are off site. Finding the cause means digging through Excel sheets, PLC CSV exports and shift logs, about 40 minutes [pending interviews], while every hour of downtime costs X [pending interviews].

凌晨停線、主管一個人翻 Excel 找原因。40 分鐘和每小時損失都還沒有訪談數字。

Solution

One Investigate button, no chat box. Gemini calls five fixed, tested queries and never writes SQL (demo data on DuckDB, swappable for BigQuery). Every step becomes an evidence card that opens to its source rows. No evidence, no conclusion: it answers “Insufficient evidence” instead of guessing.

1Alarm

03:00, Line 2 turns red; the supervisor presses Investigate.

2Evidence

Cards appear one by one (temperature, coolant flow, valve, shift log), each tracing back to source data rows.

3Action

Root cause “cooling valve CV-2 stuck” with a server-scored confidence label; one click creates a work order, opened on a phone by QR.

Gemini 只能選固定查詢，數字可回查原始列，證據不足就出灰卡。實測約 12 秒找到根因（中位數 12.6 秒，gemini-2.5-flash，docs/qa/runs）。
